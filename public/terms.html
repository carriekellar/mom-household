Work Wife is operated by Carrington Hay Kellar. It is an automated SMS assistant that helps adults coordinate small-group activities by collecting availability, proposing times, collecting confirmations, and sending reminders, changes, and cancellation notices. Support: carrie@candur.ai.

You must be at least 18 and authorized to use the phone number you enroll. Provide accurate scheduling information and enroll only yourself. An organizer's invitation does not give permission to message someone else. Keep shared plan codes within the intended group.
