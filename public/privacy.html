Privacy Policy

Work Wife · Draft dated November 8, 2026

Draft — not yet effective. Prepared for Carrington Hay Kellar's review. Finalize retention and deployment details and confirm that actual operations match this policy before launch.

Who operates Work Wife

Work Wife is operated by Carrington Hay Kellar. Contact carrie@candur.ai with privacy questions or requests.

Information we collect

When you text the service, we receive your phone number, message text, and messaging identifiers and delivery information. Messages may include your name, availability, timezone, plan title, location, and RSVP or cancellation decisions. We store plan membership and scheduling state. The enrollment flow also records consent and withdrawal information, including timestamps and the disclosure version.

Hosting and messaging providers may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, timestamps, and diagnostic logs. These draft pages do not include advertising trackers, analytics scripts, or web signup forms. We do not connect to your calendar in this version.

How information is used

We use scheduling information to interpret your requests, find shared availability, coordinate confirmations, send reminders and updates, handle opt-outs, provide support, and diagnose errors or misuse. We do not use this scheduling program to send marketing texts.

Service providers and AI

Sendblue processes incoming and outgoing pilot messages, including iMessage and possible SMS fallback. Twilio remains configured as a previous SMS provider. OpenAI processes message text and relevant scheduling context to interpret requests. The application requests that OpenAI not store Responses API outputs; this is not a promise of zero provider retention. Provider processing and retention remain subject to their applicable terms and settings.

The hosted version uses Vercel for application hosting and Supabase for database storage. These providers process information needed to operate the service. These are the providers used by the current deployment.

Information shared within a plan

Participants receive shared plan details such as the activity, proposed or confirmed time, location, and schedule changes. Organizers may receive attendance and opt-out updates. A plan code allows people to join an open plan, so share it only with intended participants. Do not send details you would not want included in scheduling context or plan updates.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Operational processing by messaging, AI, hosting, and database providers is described above. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect users, security, or legal rights.

Optional calendar downloads

If the organizer requests it, confirmed participants receive a link to a calendar file containing the plan title, time, and location. Each participant chooses whether to save it. Work Wife does not connect to, read, or directly edit your calendar. Anyone with the download link can view those plan details, so share it only with intended participants. Saved events do not automatically sync with later schedule changes or cancellations.

Retention and deletion

The prototype stores messages, plan records, delivery records, and opt-out state. It does not currently implement automatic deletion. Before launch: the operator must select and implement retention periods for these records and backups. We may retain limited records to honor opt-outs, address disputes, and meet legal obligations.

You may request access, correction, or deletion by emailing carrie@candur.ai. We may need to verify control of the relevant phone number. Requests will be handled subject to applicable law and legitimate retention requirements. Texting STOP stops messages; it does not by itself delete stored records.

Security and your choices

We use access controls and provider security features to protect information, but no system is completely secure. SMS is not an end-to-end encrypted channel. For iMessage, Sendblue and Work Wife process message contents to provide the service; messages are not private from these service providers. Avoid sending passwords, payment information, medical details, or other sensitive information.

Reply STOP to opt out of scheduling texts. Reply HELP for help. You may also contact the support email above. Privacy rights may vary based on where you live.

Adults and children's information

Work Wife is for adults aged 18 or older. We do not ask children to enroll or ask for children's names. Adults should describe plans without children's identifying or sensitive details. Contact us if a child has provided personal information so we can review and address it.

Changes

We will update the date and publish changes here. Material changes will be communicated as appropriate. This draft has no effective date until approved for launch.
